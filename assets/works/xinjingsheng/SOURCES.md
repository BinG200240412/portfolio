# 心·境·声 / Heart · Space · Sound

Source: the author-supplied `蔡正阳作品集.pdf`, in the supplied 心静声 folder.
The project title is printed as `心.境.声`. The English title is an editorial translation.

Only pages 1–8 belong to this project. Page 9 begins 千里江山 and is excluded.
Project dates printed on page 1: 2021.08–2021.12. Project lead: 蔡正阳.

Images are extracted from the PDF's embedded image objects. `manifest.json`
records page and xref provenance. The sketch is rotated to its orientation in
the PDF; transparent model margins are trimmed. Full-resolution image exports
remain in `originals/`; photographs use high-quality JPEG compression to avoid
large PNG downloads on zoom. The page initially requests small WebP previews.

The opening bell/water images are visual intent, not evidence of the final
installation. Physical assembly and installation images come from page 8.
No new images, test results, clinical claims or personal responsibilities are
invented. The heart-rate diagram reproduces the three ranges described on
page 7 (60–80, 80–100 and 100–140 BPM); each maps to a fixed servo speed,
not a claimed beat-by-beat synchronization.

Rebuild images with `python3 scripts/build-xinjingsheng-assets.py --source INPUT.pdf`.

The two installation recordings are from the video QR on page 8:
`http://qr71.cn/o8w7OB/qr3FYk5`. The full films retain the original 1280×720
video and audio streams, remuxed for progressive playback. Small muted previews
load only when visible; clicking opens the original recording with sound and
playback controls. `films/manifest.json` records durations and source hashes.
